The incentive — why a fan comes back every session

🏁 Garage Pass tiers

Free fans follow & get live timing. Garage Pass (paid / team-comped) unlocks stint predictions, historical trends, and a shout-out wall. Revenue the team shares in.

🎯 Predict & Points

Call quali position, pit windows, or the fastest sector. Correct calls earn Paddock Points and climb a per-team leaderboard through the season.

🔥 Streaks & badges

Show up for every session, follow through a full endurance race, or spot a new track record — earn badges that make fans feel like part of the crew.

💬 Crew Radio moments

Teams push a cleared radio clip or "why we pitted" note. Fans get the insider feel — the team controls exactly what's shared.

🏆 Fan of the Race

Top predictors & most-active fans get surfaced to the team — grid walks, meet-and-greets, signed gear. Cheap for the team, huge for the fan.

📈 Value back to the team

Every follow, prediction and Garage Pass is audience data & sponsor value the team owns — a measurable fanbase to take to partners.

Fans must be associated — to a team they follow, or as an unaffiliated spectator. Either way they have a home in Paddock, and the team gets a growing, measurable following.
